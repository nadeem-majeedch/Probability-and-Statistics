## Role in this course

Engr. Dr. Majeed is the author and instructor of *Probability and Statistics for
Data Science*, taught in the BS Data Science programme at the Department of Data
Science (PUCIT), University of the Punjab, Lahore. He designed the curriculum, the
worked examples, the exercises and the assessment scheme in this repository, and he
maintains it.

## Why this course is written for self-study

The material is written so that a student can complete it without an instructor
present. That decision shapes everything about it:

- Every result is derived in the module where it is used, with every step justified.
- Every worked example is numerically complete, including the arithmetic.
- Every exercise and quiz item has a full solution, because there is nobody to ask.
- Every topic is introduced through intuition and a figure before its formal
  statement, because a definition met without a picture rarely sticks.
- Simulations are used throughout, because a result you can reproduce numerically is
  a result you can trust.

If you are using this course in a taught class, the structure maps onto a 16-week
semester and the assessment scheme can be adopted directly — see the
[syllabus](../syllabus.md).

## Contact

Corrections, questions and reports of errors are welcome through the repository's
issue tracker:

<https://github.com/nadeem-majeedch/Probability-and-Statistics/issues>

When reporting a mathematical error, please give the module number, the section, and
what you believe the correct statement is. Errata in formulas are treated as
correctness bugs and are fixed in the open.

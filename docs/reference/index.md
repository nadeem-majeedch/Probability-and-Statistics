## The order that works

**During the course**, keep the [formula sheet](formula-sheet.md) open beside the chapter. When a
formula appears, find it on the sheet and check that the assumptions match the situation.

**Before a tutorial**, read the matching [quick sheet](revision/quick-sheets.md) for two minutes.
It is a map, not a substitute.

**Before an examination**, work through the [identities](revision/identities.md), then the
[mistakes](revision/mistakes.md), then the [conceptual questions](revision/questions.md), then the
[exam guide](revision/exam-guide.md). That order moves from tools to traps to understanding to
strategy.

**When an answer is marked wrong**, go to [common mistakes](revision/mistakes.md) first. Most errors
are one of those forty.

## A caution about formula sheets

A formula sheet is a safety net, not a method. Every entry states its assumptions because a formula
applied outside them produces a confident wrong answer. If you cannot say when a formula does *not*
apply, you do not yet know it well enough to use it.
